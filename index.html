🏛️ What is Lok Sabha?
The Lok Sabha (House of the People) has 543 directly elected members. Elections are held every 5 years under Article 81 of the Constitution. The majority party/alliance forms the government.
📋 FPTP System
India uses First-Past-The-Post (FPTP) voting. The candidate with the most votes in each constituency wins the seat, regardless of whether they get a majority.
🗳️ Election Commission
The Election Commission of India (ECI), established in 1950, is an autonomous constitutional body responsible for conducting free and fair elections across India.
📊 2024 General Election
The 2024 Indian General Election saw 642 million voters cast ballots across 7 phases. It is the largest election ever held in human history, with 1.5 billion people eligible.
